    § V

    Read it like counsel.

    Upload a judgment, notice or contract. Wakeel marks what matters and writes a headnote, the way a law report does.

    Illustrative sample
    In the Court of the Sessions JudgePetition under Section 22-A(6), Cr.P.C.

    The petitioner states that on reporting the theft of his motorcycle at the police station, the officer in charge declined to record the information, advising him to return at a later date.

    It is trite that where information discloses the commission of a cognizable offence, the officer in charge is bound to reduce it into writing under Section 154 of the Code; he is not to sit in judgment over its truth at that stage.

    Accordingly, the SHO concerned is directed to record the statement of the petitioner and proceed in accordance with law.

    Headnote by Wakeel

    At a glance

    HELDInformation disclosing a cognizable offence must be recorded; its truth is tested later, not at the police station door.

    In plain words: if the police station refuses, the Sessions Judge (as Justice of Peace) can direct it to register your FIR.

    § VII

    Questions before the bench

    Is it free?

    Yes — legal chat, know-your-rights guidance and basic document understanding are free for the public. Paid plans add study, drafting and professional tools.

    Does it replace an advocate?

    No. It helps you understand and prepare. Representation in court requires a licensed advocate — you will simply arrive better prepared.

    How can I trust the answer?

    Every answer shows the law it relies on so it can be checked. Before important decisions, always confirm with an advocate.

    Can I ask in Roman Urdu, or by voice?

    Yes — Urdu, English, Roman Urdu or a mix of them, however you naturally speak.

    Does it work outside Pakistan?

    Yes. It is built around Pakistani law, with guidance for the GCC and 15+ other countries.
