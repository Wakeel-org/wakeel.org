Legalese, translated

English legal paper. Your language back.

Most legal documents in Pakistan are written in dense English. Wakeel turns them into plain English or Urdu — and tells you which line to worry about.

EnglishاردوRoman UrduVoice
Tenancy agreement · Clause 9

Notwithstanding anything contained hereinabove, the Lessee shall, upon the expiry or sooner determination of the tenancy, peaceably surrender vacant possession of the demised premises to the Lessor, failing which the Lessee shall be liable to pay mesne profits at double the monthly rent for every month or part thereof.

In plain words

When the rental ends — or if it's ended early — you must leave the house empty and hand it back calmly. If you stay on, you may owe double rent for every month, even part of a month.
