"Police refused to register my FIR. What can I do?"

Under Section 154 Cr.P.C., an officer in charge of a police station must register an FIR for a cognizable offence. If refused, you can write to the Superintendent of Police, or approach a Magistrate under Section 22-A/22-B Cr.P.C. to direct registration.

✓ Source-aware

"Explain PPC Section 420 in simple terms."

Section 420 PPC covers cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property — deceiving someone into handing over property, money, or a valuable document. It's a common section cited in fraud complaints.

✓ Source-aware

"My landlord won't return my security deposit."

Tenancy rules vary by province, but landlords generally must return a deposit minus documented damage. Send a written demand notice first; if unresolved, file with the rent tribunal in your district.

Before you ask

Frequently asked questions.

Is my document data safe when I upload it?

Document processing is encrypted, and Wakeel is built to remind users to avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive data. Full detail lives in the Privacy Policy — worth a read before uploading anything you wouldn't want stored.

What if Wakeel's answer is wrong?

Every answer is source-aware and framed as legal information, not final advice. Wakeel builds in reminders to verify citations and consult a licensed advocate before filing, signing, paying, or appearing in court.

Do I need to pay to get real value?

No — the Free plan includes a daily legal-question allowance, Know Your Rights guidance, and basic document handling with no payment required. Paid tiers add document depth, drafting, and team workflows for heavier use.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Google?

Wakeel is grounded specifically in Pakistani statutes and procedure, answers in English or Urdu, and has its legal accuracy reviewed by a practicing advocate — rather than drawing generically from the open web.

Does Wakeel replace a lawyer?

No. Wakeel provides legal information and research support. It does not provide final legal advice, does not guarantee outcomes, and does not replace consultation with a licensed advocate.

Is Wakeel a chatbot, or does it do more?

More. Wakeel works agentically — it breaks your question into research steps, checks it against Pakistani statutes and case law, and only then composes an answer, instead of generating a single guessed reply the way a plain chatbot does.
