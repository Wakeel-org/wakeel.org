Documents

Dense paperwork, explained line by line.

Notices, contracts, rent agreements, nikah nama, judgments — up to 100+ pages. Tap a highlight to see what it really means.

1
The demand

This is a claim, not a proven debt. Check it against your agreement and payment records.

2
The warning

A notice is not a case. A purely money dispute is usually a civil matter, whatever the letter says.

3
The deadline

Note the date you received it and keep the envelope. Reply in writing — ideally through an advocate — with proof of delivery.

4
The presumption

Silence doesn't legally admit the claim, but it can be used against you later. Replying is the safer path.

Trust

What Wakeel is — and isn't. Plainly.

In law, false confidence does real harm. So we state our limits up front.

Wakeel is

  • Legal information and research support, in plain language
  • Source-aware — answers point to the statute or section
  • The best way to prepare before you meet an advocate
  • A faster research and drafting desk for professionals

Wakeel is not

  • A replacement for a licensed advocate
  • Final legal advice, or a guarantee of any outcome
  • A lawyer–client relationship
  • Representation in court

Questions

What people ask before they start.

Is it really free?

Yes. Basic legal chat and know-your-rights guidance are free. Paid plans add study, drafting and professional tools.

Can I ask in Urdu — or Roman Urdu?

Yes. Write or speak in Urdu, English or a mix of both, the way you naturally would.

Can this replace my lawyer?

No. Wakeel helps you understand your situation and prepare. For court matters and important decisions you need a licensed advocate — and you'll arrive far better prepared.

Is what I share kept private?

Your conversations and documents are yours. Our privacy policy explains exactly how data is handled and stored.

I live outside Pakistan. Does it still help?

Yes. Overseas Pakistanis use it for property, inheritance and family matters back home, and there's guidance for the GCC and 15+ other countries.
