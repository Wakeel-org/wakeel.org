How Wakeel reads the law

Section 154, Code of Criminal Procedure

Live example

Every information relating to the commission of a cognizable offence1 if given orally to an officer incharge of a police station, shall be reduced to writing2 by him or under his direction, and be read over to the informant3; and every such information, whether given in writing or reduced to writing as aforesaid, shall be signed by the person giving it4, and the substance thereof shall be entered in a book5 to be kept by such officer in such form as the Provincial Government may prescribe in this behalf.

In plain words1 / 5

“cognizable offence”

A serious crime — like theft, robbery or fraud — where the police can act without first getting a court's permission.

      Documents

      Before you sign anything, let Wakeel read it.

      Upload a rent agreement, a job offer or a legal notice. Wakeel marks what's risky, what's normal and what to watch — right in the margin, where you can see it.

      RENT AGREEMENT

      Lahore · 1 November 2026 · Landlord: Mr. K. Mehmood · Tenant: Mr. A. Raza

      !3.The monthly rent shall be Rs. 50,000/- payable in advance by the 5th of each month. The rent shall stand increased by 10% every year.

      ✕4.The Tenant has paid a security deposit of Rs. 150,000/-. The security deposit shall not be refundable under any circumstances.

      ✓7.Either party may terminate this agreement by giving one month's notice in writing.

      ✕9.The Tenant shall bear all repairs of whatever nature during the tenancy.

      Landlord ____________Tenant ____________
      2 risks 1 to watch 1 standard
      !

      Clause 3 · Watch

      Your rent rises every year: Rs 50,000, then 55,000, then 60,500. Budget for it.

      ✕

      Clause 4 · Risk

      A deposit that is never refundable is one-sided and unusual. Negotiate this before you sign.

      ✓

      Clause 7 · Standard

      One month's notice for either side is standard and fair.

      ✕

      Clause 9 · Risk

      “All repairs of whatever nature” could include major structural work. Ask to limit it to day-to-day repairs.

      Questions

      Asked often. Answered plainly.

      Is Wakeel a lawyer? Is this legal advice?

      No. Wakeel provides legal information and research support. It helps you understand your situation and prepare — but decisions about a real case should be made with a licensed advocate.

      Can I ask in Urdu or Roman Urdu?

      Yes. Type or speak in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, and choose the language you'd like the answer in.

      How accurate is it?

      Wakeel answers from Pakistani statutes and reported judgments and shows the source for every point, so you — or your advocate — can check it. Like any research tool it can make mistakes, which is exactly why the sources are always visible.

      I'm not a legal expert. Will I understand the answers?

      That's the point. Every answer has a plain-words version and a professional brief, side by side. You can also enlarge the text or listen to answers.

      Is my information private?

      Your conversations and documents are encrypted and never sold. You can delete your history at any time.

      Does it cover laws outside Pakistan?

      Wakeel is built Pakistan-first, across federal and provincial law. Overseas Pakistanis use it for matters back home, and coverage of other jurisdictions is growing.

      Is it really free?

      Yes. The Free plan includes daily questions with sourced answers. Paid plans add depth for students and professionals.
